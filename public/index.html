Assumptions & sources

Projection from your statements + stated plans; Closing Disclosure and actual payoff quote govern. Assumes relo covers commissions/seller costs (net = price − payoff), proceeds land on the sale date, loss credit 14 days later, AutoFortiva settled in full 8/20/26, the Vivint contract bought out 7 days before the sale at 50% of the months remaining on the 6/21/25 60-month term (drafts stop after that), Midland utilities end at sale with one final trailing bill, and the 6-mo car insurance renewal ($940) recurs each March and September. Car payoff uses simple interest at 6.74% actual/365 from the $40,862.63 principal standing after the 8/16/26 payment, with no further $500 extra payments. The new-home payment steps from $2,691.97 to $3,180.72 on Jan 1, 2027 (escrow re-set for $6,000/yr property tax, no shortage spread). The Midland escrow was set at the mid-May 2025 closing against a bare-lot assessment, so it funds insurance ($1,329/yr) and land-only tax ($567.84/yr) plus $28.75 PMI; the first assessment including the house is billed Jan 31, 2027, and PennyMac's annual analysis resets the payment from June 2027 with the shortage spread over 12 months. The March bonus is shown net at 65.60% of gross (22% federal supplemental + 6.2% + 1.45% + 4.75% Oklahoma) and repeats each year with the $5,000 IRS payment. 2026 and 2027 are modelled from statements; from 2028 pay rises 3%/yr and costs inflate 3%/yr (not the mortgage P&I or car payment, which are fixed). Depreciation recapture on a rented-then-sold home is straight line over 27.5 years at 29.75% (25% federal + 4.75% Oklahoma, which applies because you are an OK resident even though the house is in Texas). Utilities and the $6,000 tax figure are estimates.
